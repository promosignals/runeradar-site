What's in the server

Free for everyone. Useful whether you flip full-time or just want to buy upgrades for less and sell your drops for more.

  • Price lookups

    Use /price or !price on any tradeable item. Less-traded items are flagged when their price is stale.

  • Hourly movers

    A regular post of the items that moved the most, skipping prices that haven't actually traded recently.

  • Live dashboard

    A channel that keeps refreshing with a current snapshot of the market.

  • Works in DMs

    Every command also works in a private message with the bot, so you can check prices without posting in a channel.

RuneRadar Pro

Pro adds item watches: pick the items you care about and get a ping when their margin or price moves enough to matter. It also unlocks the full dashboard.

Subscribe directly inside the Discord server. Payment is handled by Discord, and your Pro role is applied automatically.
